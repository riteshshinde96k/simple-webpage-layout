About the Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal, located in Agra, India, is one of the most beautiful monuments in the world. Built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife Mumtaz Mahal, it stands as a symbol of eternal love and beauty. The intricate marble work, lush gardens, and architectural brilliance make it a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a must-visit destination.
