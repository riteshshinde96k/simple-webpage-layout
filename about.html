About Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is an iconic symbol of love, recognized as one of the most beautiful structures in the world. Located in Agra, India, it is a white marble mausoleum built by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his beloved wife, Mumtaz Mahal.

Key Facts

  • Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India
  • Built: 1632–1653
  • Architectural Style: Mughal architecture, with Persian, Ottoman Turkish, and Indian influences
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site: Yes, since 1983
  • Visitors: Over 7-8 million annually

Why it is Famous

The Taj Mahal is renowned for its stunning architecture, intricate carvings, and beautiful gardens. The symmetrical design, the reflective pools, and the white marble that changes color depending on the time of day make it a marvel of architectural achievement. It is often referred to as a "symbol of eternal love" due to the emotional story behind its creation.
